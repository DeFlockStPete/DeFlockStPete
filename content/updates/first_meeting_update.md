### Huge Turnout!
Our first public meeting at the Obama Library had a better turnout than we could have possibly
expected! So much so that unfortunately some people had to be turned away at the door. We're incredibly
sorry to any who couldn't get in, especially those who had RSVP'd beforehand. This will definitely be
a learning experience and we'll make sure to look for a bigger venue, provide a remote option, and
be stricter about RSVPs when space is tight at future events. In the meantime, you can download the
slides using the link above, and if you have any questions that you weren't able to ask, please send
us an email at [DeFlockStPete@proton.me](mailto:DeFlockStPete@proton.me)!

### Where we go from here
It was amazing to see so much of the community come together to stand up against mass surveilance! Not
only  was there great energy, but there were also so many thoughtful questions and ideas. Clearly
people have been thinking about this issue and looking for an outlet to express their feelings. We
hope that we can provide that outlet and help to build upon this momentum. Key to doing that is
getting the community connected and talking.

With this in mind, we are looking for everyone who is interested in volunteering and/or helping to
organize future events to reach out by email (see email above). At this point we have a small Signal
chat with folks who have been involved in DeFlock St Pete and it would be great to either expand this
chat or move to another platform with everyone interested in getting involved. As we expand to a larger
chat, we should note that this increases the chances that communications will be leaked, so we ask
anyone interested in joining to treat this space as if it is public.

For those who are interested in staying up-to-date with the campaign, but not necessarily becoming
volunteers, we will continue to use the email list and advertise on other platforms. Keep an eye out in
the near future, as we will be using the coming days to plan what's next for DeFlock St Pete!
