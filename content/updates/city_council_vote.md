The anti-surveillance movement has more momentum than ever, and our elected
officials are starting to take notice!

This week, city council voted to bring a discussion about our Flock contract
to the Public Services and Infrastructure Committee! This business item was
brought forward by Council Member Richie Floyd and was approved unanimously.
This is only the first step to getting Flock cameras off our streets, but it
opens up the door for us to make our voices heard!

The Public Services and Infrastructure Committee is made up of council members
Lisset Hanewicz, Mike Harting, Richie Floyd, and Copley Gerdes. We encourage
you to reach out to these council members to let them know that you want to see
Flock cameras removed from the city. Given that the vote was unanimous, we also
encourage you to thank council for their support. For information on how to contact
city council members, see our [get involved page]({{< relref "get_involved" >}}).
